Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Boundary processes information in its Android scoring app, account registration, community, public match pages and merchandise shop.

Information we process

Accounts: Clerk processes sign-in details, verified phone numbers and account sessions. Boundary stores the account identifier, cricket display name and a keyed hash of the registered phone number for exact phone lookup. Phone numbers are not listed in a public directory. An authorised, linked scoring app can find a registered player's name and account identifier by entering that player's phone number.

Device linking: We store a hash of the device session token, a hash of the requesting network address, expiry times and lookup counters. A pending link lasts ten minutes; an approved device session lasts up to thirty days unless revoked. The app stores its session token privately on the device and excludes it from its declared Android backup set.

Cricket and community: Teams, squads, match events, named officials, fielding credits, scores, statistics, community posts and replies may contain information you or other users enter. Public match snapshots and community posts are publicly accessible. The scoring device stores its match journal locally; private cloud recovery journals are encrypted separately from public score snapshots.

Orders: For an order we store the recipient name, delivery address, verified contact phone, items, amount, payment provider references, status and timestamps. Razorpay processes payment details; Boundary stores gateway identifiers and confirmation/refund states, not full card numbers, CVVs or UPI PINs.

Camera, microphone and local files

The app requests camera or microphone access for features you choose, such as voice scoring, scorebook scanning, replay and streaming. Offline voice recognition and an optional speaker profile run on the device; the speaker profile is stored locally. Scorebook scanning uses an on-device text recognition library. Replay recordings and exports are local unless you share them. A live stream sends camera and any enabled microphone audio to the streaming endpoint you configure. Android sharing sends selected content to the app or recipient you choose.

Why the information is used

We use this information to authenticate accounts, verify player eligibility, link your app, calculate scores and statistics, publish match updates, show community content, prevent abuse, reserve stock, process orders and payments, reconcile refunds and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal information or use your private recovery journal for advertising.

Providers and sharing

Boundary uses Vercel for hosting and request processing, Neon for server databases, Clerk for authentication, and Razorpay for payments. The shop loads Google Fonts, and the provider scripts needed for sign-in and checkout. These services receive the connection and device information needed to deliver their features and may process data in countries other than yours. Their own privacy policies also apply: Vercel, Neon, Clerk and Razorpay.

Order delivery details are intended for the merchant and authorised fulfilment/delivery providers when sales open. Public scores, player names on scorecards, posts and replies are visible to other users. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

Browser storage and security

Authentication uses provider cookies and session mechanisms. The shop uses browser storage for a product bag and a checkout request identifier. Clearing this storage removes the local bag but does not delete saved orders or payments. Boundary sends service requests over HTTPS, checks account sessions server-side, and verifies payment ownership and capture before recording a paid order. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and your choices

Local scoring data remains on your device until removed through app or device controls. Server records are retained as needed to provide the feature, investigate issues and meet legal, accounting or order obligations. Existing public cricket records may include other participants' history; correction or deletion requests need to account for those records and applicable obligations.

You can sign out, unlink the Android device, manage available account details through Clerk's account menu, remove your own community posts, and request access, correction or deletion of your information through support. Signing out or uninstalling does not automatically delete cloud data. Contact support to withdraw player registration or request a data export or deletion. We may need to verify ownership before acting.

Children, contact and policy changes

Minors should use Boundary with a parent or guardian's permission and supervision. Purchases require an adult authorised to pay. Please contact support if a child has supplied information without appropriate permission.

The store support contact appears in the shop once configured. Until then, ask the project maintainer for a private contact channel. The issue tracker is public: do not post your phone number, address, identification, private recovery code or payment details there. This policy will be updated when data practices change; the revision date appears above.
