Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

This policy explains what information Boundary collects in its Android scoring app, player registration, community, public match pages and Club Shop, and how we use, store and protect it.

In short

  • We collect what we need to run accounts, scoring, the community and orders. That includes your verified Google email, display name, match data, posts and delivery details.
  • For player lookup we store a keyed hash of your Google email. Email addresses are not listed in any public directory.
  • Razorpay handles card and UPI details. We never receive your full card number, CVV or UPI PIN.
  • Public scores, names on scorecards and community posts can be seen by anyone.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not use advertising or analytics cookies.
  • You can ask for access, correction or deletion of your information through support.

This summary is for convenience only. The full policy below applies.

1. Who we are and what this covers

Boundary is operated by [Registered business name], [Registered postal address] ("we", "us"). We decide how and why the personal information described here is processed.

This policy covers the Boundary Android app, this website, player registration, the community, public match pages and the Club Shop. Third-party services that we link to or use have their own privacy policies, listed in section 7.

2. Information we collect

Account and sign-in

Clerk, our sign-in provider, processes your Google account identity, email address and sign-in sessions. Clerk also holds any name or username you add to your account. We use that name as the author name on your community posts.

Player registration

When you register as a player, we store your account identifier, cricket display name, unique public username, a keyed hash of your verified Google email, the registration time and the version of the terms you accepted. A linked scoring app can find you by exact username, Google email or player code and receives your display name, username and account identifier. Your Google email itself is not shown in any public directory. WhatsApp invitations open a message composer on your device; Boundary does not read your WhatsApp contacts or send messages automatically.

Device linking

When you link the Android app, we store a hash of the device session token, a hash of the requesting network address, expiry times and lookup counters. The app keeps its session token privately on your phone and excludes it from its declared Android backup set.

Cricket and match data

Teams, squads, match events, named officials, fielding credits, scores and statistics can contain information that you or other users enter. When a scorer publishes a match, a public snapshot is stored and anyone can view it. The scoring device keeps its full match journal locally. A private cloud recovery journal, if used, is encrypted separately from the public snapshot.

Community

For community posts and replies we store your account identifier, author name, the category, title, city and text you enter, and the time you posted. Posts and replies are public.

Orders and delivery

For a shop order we store the recipient name, delivery address, city, state, PIN code, your Google account email, the items, amounts and delivery charge, Razorpay order, payment and refund references, order status, timestamps and the terms version you accepted.

Support messages

If you contact us, we keep your message, your contact details and any order reference you include so we can reply.

Technical and security information

Our hosting provider processes connection information, such as your IP address and browser details, to deliver and protect the service. Our rate limits store keyed hashes of IP addresses or account identifiers, never the plain values.

On your phone

  • The app asks for camera or microphone access only for features you choose, such as voice scoring, scorebook scanning, replay and live streaming.
  • Offline voice recognition and the optional speaker profile run on the phone. The speaker profile stays on the phone.
  • Scorebook scanning uses on-device text recognition.
  • Replay recordings and exports stay on the phone unless you share them.
  • A live stream sends camera video and any enabled microphone audio to the streaming endpoint you configure.
  • The profile name, email and photo you add in the app are stored privately on the phone. Boundary does not upload them.
  • If you turn on Android backup, Android may back up the local match database and scorebook files under Google's terms. The account session token, your profile details and photo, and the speaker profile are excluded.
  • Android sharing sends the content you select to the app or person you choose.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • sign you in, verify your Google identity and confirm player eligibility for new matches;
  • link your Android app and let it find registered players for squad selection;
  • calculate scores and statistics and publish match updates;
  • show community posts and replies;
  • reserve stock, process orders and payments, arrange delivery, and handle cancellations, returns and refunds;

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not use your private recovery journal for anything other than restoring your match.

4. Consent and legal basis

We process personal information with your consent, which you give when you register, accept the terms at checkout, or choose to use a feature. We also process it where the law allows without separate consent, for example to complete an order you placed, to meet legal obligations, or to keep the service secure.

We handle personal information in line with applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules and, as its provisions apply, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. This policy is a general description and is not legal advice.

You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting support. We will then stop processing for that purpose, although some features may no longer work for you. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done, or records we must keep by law.

6. Payment processing

Razorpay processes all shop payments. You enter card, UPI, net banking or wallet details in Razorpay's checkout window, not on Boundary.

We never receive or store your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN or banking passwords.

  • What we send to Razorpay. The order amount and currency, our order reference, and your recipient name and Google account email to prefill checkout.
  • What we receive from Razorpay. The Razorpay order and payment identifiers, payment status, amount and currency, and any refund identifier and refund status.
  • How we confirm payment. Our server checks the payment signature, the order owner, the amount, the currency and a captured payment with Razorpay before it marks an order as paid.

Razorpay may collect further payment and device information to process payments and prevent fraud. Its use of that information is covered by the Razorpay privacy policy.

  • With linked scoring apps. A linked app that enters a registered player's exact username, Google email or player code receives that player's display name, username and account identifier.

9. Storage and security

Our server records are stored in a Neon Postgres database. Vercel currently runs our server functions in the United States, and our providers may store or process data in countries other than India.

Our safeguards include:

  • HTTPS for all service requests;
  • server-side checks of every signed-in request;
  • a keyed hash of registered Google emails for player lookup, so the lookup table does not hold the email addresses themselves;
  • hashes, not plain values, for device session tokens, network addresses and rate-limit subjects;
  • separate encryption for private match recovery journals;
  • payment checks of signature, owner, amount, currency and capture before an order is marked as paid;
  • rate limits on device linking, player lookup, community posting and shop requests.

New orders keep your Google email in readable form for order communication. Historical phone hashes and order contact fields may remain in records created before Google registration replaced phone registration; they are no longer used for player lookup. No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

11. Children

If you are under 18, use Boundary only with the permission and supervision of a parent or legal guardian. Purchases require an adult who is authorised to pay. We do not track children's behaviour, and we do not show advertising to anyone.

If you believe a child has given us information without a parent or guardian's permission, contact support and we will delete it.

12. Your rights and choices

Depending on the law that applies, you can ask us to:

  • give you a summary of the personal information we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or update inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • delete your information, unless we must keep it by law;
  • stop processing that relies on your consent;
  • handle a complaint through our grievance officer;
  • let a person you nominate exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to act.

Things you can do yourself

  • Sign out, or log out of the Android app to end its device link.
  • Remove your own community posts.
  • Manage available account details in Clerk's account menu.
  • Clear the shop bag by clearing your browser storage.
  • Erase all Boundary data on a phone with the app's Clear data option.

Deleting your account

In the Android app, the Delete account option logs the phone out, removes your profile details and photo from the phone, and prepares an email asking support to delete your registered player record. You can also email support directly with your account details.

We verify that the request comes from the account owner before acting. We then delete your player registration and device links and close your sign-in account. We keep order records that tax and accounting law requires. Match scorecards that other scorers saved may still show the name they entered, and correction or deletion requests for those records have to take account of other participants.

Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete cloud data. We respond to verified requests within one month.
