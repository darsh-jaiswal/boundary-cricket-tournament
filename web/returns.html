Return & Exchange Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

If something is not right with your Boundary order, you can request a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery. This page explains which items qualify, how to send them back and when you get your money.

In short

  • Request a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery.
  • Items must be unused and unwashed, with original tags and packaging, and you need your order ID.
  • Personalised items, final-sale items and caps with a broken hygiene seal cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged, defective or wrong.
  • Damaged, defective or wrong item? Tell us within 48 hours with photos and we replace it free of charge.
  • Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Razorpay.
  • One exchange per item, for a different size or colour, subject to stock.

This summary is for convenience only. The full policy below applies.

1. Overview

This policy applies to products bought from the Boundary Club Shop and delivered in India. It forms part of our Terms & Conditions. Each order follows the version of this policy that was in force when the order was placed.

This policy adds to your rights under consumer protection law. It does not reduce them.

2. The 7-day window

  • Request a return or exchange within 7 calendar days of the delivery date shown in the courier's tracking. For example, if your order is delivered on 10 November, request it by the end of 17 November.
  • After we approve your request, hand the item to the courier or ship it within [X] days.
  • We cannot accept requests made after the window closes, except where the law gives you a right to a remedy for defective goods.

3. Eligibility conditions

To qualify for a return or exchange, an item must meet all of these conditions:

  • It is unused and unwashed, and has not been worn beyond briefly trying it on to check the fit.
  • It is free from stains, marks, odours such as perfume or smoke, hair and alterations.
  • Its original tags are still attached, and it comes in its original packaging.
  • Everything that came with it is included.
  • You give your order ID or another proof of purchase.
  • It has not been damaged by misuse, an accident or washing against the care instructions.
  • It is not listed as non-returnable in section 4.

4. Items that cannot be returned

Unless they arrived damaged, defective or wrong, we cannot accept returns or exchanges of:

  • personalised or customised items, such as jerseys printed with a name, number or team logo at your request;
  • items marked "Final sale" or "Non-returnable" on the product page or at checkout;
  • caps, other headwear and any item supplied with a hygiene seal, once the seal is broken. This rule applies only to products whose product page says a hygiene seal applies;
  • items damaged by misuse, an accident, washing against the care instructions, or normal wear and tear;
  • items returned without their tags or original packaging, or after the 7-day window.

5. How to request a return or exchange

  1. Email us within 7 days of delivery. Write to darshjaiswal009.xi@gmail.com with "Return" or "Exchange" and your order ID in the subject line. Your order ID is shown in My orders in the shop.
  2. Tell us what and why. Give the item name, size, colour and quantity, and the reason. For an exchange, say which size or colour you want. For a damaged, defective or wrong item, attach clear photos of the item, the problem, the tags, the packaging and the shipping label.
  3. Wait for approval. We reply within [X business days]. If reverse pickup is available for your PIN code, we arrange it. Otherwise, we send you the return address.
  4. Pack and send the item. Pack it securely in its original packaging with the tags attached, and include a note with your order ID. If you ship it yourself, use a tracked service and keep the receipt and tracking number.

Please do not send items back before we approve the request, as we may not be able to match them to your order. Never include card details, one-time codes or UPI PINs in your email.

6. Inspection

We inspect returned items within [X business days] of receiving them, and email you the result.

  • If the item meets the conditions in section 3, we approve your refund or send your exchange.
  • If it does not, we email you the reasons with photos and send the item back to you. The delivery charge for sending a rejected item back is [₹X / free].

7. Refunds

  • We refund approved returns to your original payment method through Razorpay within [X business days after approval]. We cannot refund in cash or to a different account.
  • Your bank or payment provider may take additional time to show the refund. The refund status appears in My orders, and the status reported by Razorpay is the one that applies.
  • We refund the price you paid for the returned items. If you return only part of an order, we refund only those items.
  • We refund the original delivery charge in full if the item was damaged, defective or wrong, or if the order is cancelled before dispatch. For other returns, the original delivery charge is not refunded.
  • For a change-of-mind return, the return shipping charge in section 10 is deducted from the refund.

8. Exchanges

  • You can exchange an item for a different size or colour of the same product at the same price.
  • Exchanges depend on stock. If the size or colour you want is unavailable, we offer a refund instead.
  • Each item can be exchanged once. The replacement cannot be exchanged again, but you can still report it as damaged, defective or wrong under section 9.
  • To change to a different product or material, return the item for a refund and place a new order.
  • We dispatch the replacement after the original item passes inspection.

9. Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your item arrives damaged or defective, or is not what you ordered, report it within 48 hours of delivery.

  • Email darshjaiswal009.xi@gmail.com with your order ID and clear photos of the item, the problem, the tags, the packaging and the shipping label. An unboxing video helps if you have one.
  • If the parcel looks damaged or opened when it arrives, note it on the courier's delivery record or refuse the parcel, and tell us.
  • Once we confirm the problem, we send a free replacement. If a replacement is unavailable, or you prefer, we refund the full amount, including the delivery charge.
  • We pay all shipping costs in these cases.

If you find a problem after 48 hours but within 7 days of delivery, still contact us. We handle it as a standard return, and your legal rights for defective goods still apply.

11. Cancellations before dispatch

  • Unpaid orders. Cancel an unpaid order yourself in My orders when no payment is processing.
  • Paid orders not yet dispatched. Email support with your order ID. We cancel the order and refund the full amount, including the delivery charge, to your original payment method within [X business days after approval].
  • Dispatched orders. A dispatched order cannot be cancelled. You can request a return after delivery under this policy. If you refuse delivery of an undamaged parcel, we treat it as a change-of-mind return once it reaches us.
